# Supply-Chain-Logistics-Analytics-Dashboard | Tableau

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze delivery operations, logistics performance, delivery delays, customer ratings, delivery modes, weather impact, vehicle performance, and regional delivery performance.

The dashboard helps identify operational patterns and areas where delivery performance can be improved.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall delivery performance
- Measure on-time delivery performance
- Analyze delayed and failed deliveries
- Compare delivery partners
- Understand the impact of weather conditions
- Analyze delivery modes
- Compare delivery performance across regions
- Evaluate vehicle performance
- Analyze customer delivery ratings
- Understand delivery time and distance patterns

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Deliveries | 24,502 |
| Delayed Deliveries | 6,543 |
| On-Time Delivery | 73.30% |
| Average Delivery Time | 6.25 Hours |
| Average Delivery Rating | 3.67 / 5 |

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### Deliveries by Partner & Status
Compares delivery partners based on delivered, delayed, and failed deliveries.

### Delivery Status Overview
Provides an overall breakdown of delivery statuses.

### Delivery Cost vs Distance
Analyzes the relationship between delivery distance and delivery cost.

### Deliveries by Delivery Mode
Compares the number of deliveries across different delivery modes.

### Delivery Status by Weather
Examines how weather conditions affect delivery outcomes.

### Average Delay by Weather Condition
Compares average delivery delay across different weather conditions.

### Delayed Deliveries by Region
Identifies regions with higher numbers of delayed deliveries.

### Average Delivery Time by Vehicle
Compares average delivery time across vehicle types.

### Average Rating by Partner
Compares customer delivery ratings across delivery partners.
